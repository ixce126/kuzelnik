Ashen Reach

WASD move, Space roll, aim with the mouse. Left click basic attack, right click gauntlet surge. 1 / 2 / 3 cast abilities. Elements react: water + shock conducts, fire + water makes steam, ice + water freezes, frozen enemies shatter, three different elemental casts (fire, ice, shock, water) within 4s trigger an Overload, with a 20s cooldown. Defeat each region's guardian to break the seal. R restarts. Press ` or use the button to open the dev menu.
